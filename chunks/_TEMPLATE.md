# NNN: Short title

- **Status:** Ready | In progress | Waiting on Lauren | Done
- **Who:** agent | Lauren
- **Goal:** one sentence.
- **Inputs:** everything needed to do this, and nothing else to read.
- **Output:** what exists when this is done (a file, a script, a finding).
- **Done when:**
  - [ ] testable check
- **Out of scope:** what not to do here.

## Handoff

Written at the end of each session: what was done, what was found, where to pick up, questions for Lauren.
